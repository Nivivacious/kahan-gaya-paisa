Kahan gaya paisa?

Call a bank about a failed UPI payment and you usually get an IVR menu in English or Hindi. This helpline listens in the language you actually speak, checks your passbook, and tells you out loud what happened and when your money comes back.

Built on Saaras (speech to text), Sarvam-105B (reasoning) and Bulbul (voice) by Nivedita Prabhu.

Talk to Saathi Bank

Pick a language, tap the mic, ask about any payment on the right. Code-mixing is fine.

    Behind the last reply

    Three Sarvam API calls per turn, timed on the server.

    1. Saaras v3–Turns the caller's speech into text in her own script
    2. Sarvam-105B–Matches the complaint to a transaction and drafts a spoken answer
    3. Bulbul v3–Reads the answer back in a natural Indian voice
    Round trip–
    Saathi Bank is fictional and the passbook is sample data. Reversal timelines follow RBI's turnaround-time rules for failed UPI transactions. Your voice is sent to Sarvam's APIs to be transcribed and isn't stored by this page.
